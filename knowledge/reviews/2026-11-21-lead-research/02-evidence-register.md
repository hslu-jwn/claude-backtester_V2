## Evidence interpretation

The largest direct new **return estimate** is post-earnings selling at high-stock-compensation firms, but it is a
B− working-paper result omitted from the published article and needs a genuinely post-2020 replication. The
strongest new **causal mechanism** is Rule 201, but archive retrieval, completeness, and trigger reconciliation
remain unresolved. The largest reported forced-flow effect is fast-track IPO inclusion, conditional on public
point-in-time eligibility reconstruction. The most practical additional US corporate-action lead is the
non-overnight follow-on reversal, conditional on a public post-2015 timing and breadth audit. The strongest new
**historical anomaly** is same-calendar-month
seasonality, which needs substantially better historical share data. Same-coin cross-venue funding is not new:
it duplicates FB-037 and the best current costed evidence is adverse.
