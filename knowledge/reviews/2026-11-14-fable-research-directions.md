## 1. Summary (five lines)

1. **Not in more price signals on the 34 CFDs.** 120 ledger rows say the same thing: where a published mechanism
   exists, the sign shows up gross at 1-3 bp (0.01-0.03 R) and the cell costs more. Calendars, clocks, lead-lag,
   bar patterns, trend, daily reversal and single-commodity carry are done; more of them only adds multiplicity.
2. **Most likely in the structure of the prop challenge itself** (rows 1-3, 13): the payoff is asymmetric, the
   pass chance at zero edge is already about 33 %, and with no time limit any small net-positive stream passes if
   sized small; the binding quantity is trade count, so several weak uncorrelated streams beat one "strong" one.
3. **Power, not ideas, is the bottleneck.** A single cell with 40-130 events cannot reach t 3 at any plausible
   effect (I-67, I-70, I-71, I-73, H13f all died there). Edge can only be *shown* with breadth (many symbols on
   one rule), daily events, pooled designs, or forward logs with sequential stop rules (rows 4-7).
4. **Second most likely: Binance perpetuals** (rows 9-11, 15, 17-21): the only place where part of an effect
   replicated (the funding leg of I-73), where breadth is free (300+ perps), where two data sets on disk or free
   are unused (taker flow, open interest), and where maker fees cut the cost from 10 to about 4 bp.
5. **Goal 2 futures stay blocked on data**; the best-documented premiums that survive futures costs (diversified
   trend and carry) need one cheap data purchase the user has declined so far (row 34). Monthly or yearly events,
   crypto CFD swing and anything needing option or order-book data: no.

## 3. Arithmetic behind rows 1-3 and 13 (mine; check with the simulator)

Let risk per trade be r % of the initial balance, per-trade net mean mu and sd sigma in R, upper barrier A = target / r
and lower barrier B = max loss / r (both in R). Brownian approximation, ignoring the daily-loss rule and fat tails:

`P(target first) = (1 - exp(-2 mu B / sigma^2)) / (1 - exp(-2 mu (A + B) / sigma^2))`; at mu = 0 it is B / (A + B).
For A = B the expected trade count is (A / mu) tanh(mu A / sigma^2), or A^2 / sigma^2 at mu = 0.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | any | 50 % | 67 % | 33 % | 100 at 1 %, 400 at 0.5 % |
| -0.01 R | 0.5 % | 40 % | n/a | n/a | about 400 |
| +0.02 R | 0.5 % | 69 % | 79 % | 54 % | about 380 |
| +0.02 R | 0.25 % | 83 % | 88 % | 73 % | about 1,330 |

Readings: (1) a pass is weak evidence of edge (33 % at zero edge), so the pass itself is not a research gate;
(2) the ledger's best net values (+0.02 to +0.04 R at about 250 trades a year) pass with 55-75 %, but one stream
needs 1.5-5 years, three uncorrelated ones under a year; (3) costs enter as drift, so the cheapest cells (US30,
GER40 at about 0.01 R) lose least per unit of variance; (4) the daily-loss rule only binds when risk x trades per
day x a losing streak nears 5 %, i.e. not at 0.5 % and up to 4 trades a day. Whether sizing a zero-edge stream for
the payoff alone is allowed (the firms' terms on gambling-like trading) and wanted is the user's decision.

## 4. Would not do

- **More calendar or clock windows on CFDs, in any conditioning.** 1,452 screened cells with 0 survivors plus F4,
  F4M, I-67, I-70, H12: the family is closed and each new condition is a rescue variant.
- **Leveraged-ETF rebalancing, close-auction imbalance, dealer gamma on indices.** F2 and F2L already tested the
  price footprint (gross right, 0.5-1.3 bp, no rise on large-move days); the sharper versions need option or
  imbalance data we do not have.
- **Single-commodity carry or roll (oil, gas), commodity index roll windows on one CFD leg.** I-68's power check
  applies unchanged: monthly sd 12-15 % against under 1 % a month.
- **Monthly, quarterly or yearly single-market events** (Russell reconstitution, Nikkei dividend reinvestment,
  COMEX gold expiry, fiscal year ends, "sell in May"): 1-12 events a year cannot reach t 3 in our data.
- **Machine learning, meta-labelling, regime filters or exit engineering on existing signals** (I-36, I-49, I-50,
  I-56): they reshape an edge and there is none to reshape; they multiply looks.
- **Multi-week post-earnings drift on large caps, index inclusion effects on single stocks:** reported as gone
  (Martineau 2022; Greenwood & Sammon 2022, "The disappearing index effect"; both UNVERIFIED), and they pay swap.
- **Perp-premium or basis mean reversion at minutes with taker fees; trading the first seconds of a news release:**
  the move is the arbitrage band or the spread.
- **Triple-swap capture, swap-free account tricks, an unbooked dividend, opposite positions across accounts,
  feed / latency arbitrage, grid or martingale sizing:** about 2 bp against cost at best, and banned or at the
  firm's discretion in typical prop terms (UNVERIFIED for FTMO: row 8). Not worth an account.
- **FX option-expiry pinning, order-book or bid-density ideas, CME gap fills in bitcoin:** no data, or a gap-fill
  claim of the kind study 11.2 showed to be random-walk touching.
- **Multi-day holds on crypto CFDs and CFD carry baskets:** swap (about 8 bp a night) and no swap history (B5).
- **The regime-balancing portfolio layer before two streams with forward evidence exist.**
- **COT as the next download** (HANDOFF's suggestion): I-74 already shows the tradeable part is below cost and the
  venue rule needs futures prices we lack (rows 33, 37).
