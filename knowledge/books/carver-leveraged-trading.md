## Method notes

- Include both transaction costs and holding costs when assessing net returns; the book treats these as separate components. (LT p.70)
- Treat a backtested Sharpe ratio as an uncertain estimate, especially when comparing instruments with different history lengths. (LT p.75)
- Compare trading speed with the available cost budget: Carver’s suggested “speed limit” allocates one-third of expected pre-cost Sharpe to costs. This is his heuristic, not an observed event return. (LT p.78)
- Scaling forecast strength is a position-sizing choice and needs calibration; it does not establish an independent return effect. (LT p.246)
