TIER 1 - get these first (forced buyers and sellers, futures, rates: where our only working edges come from)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Antti Ilmanen - Expected Returns (Wiley 2011)
    Every known return source with numbers: carry, roll, seasonality, rebalancing flows. The standard map.
 2. Lasse Heje Pedersen - Efficiently Inefficient (Princeton 2015; newer edition if there is one)
    How hedge funds earn from forced trades; interviews; why an edge survives or not.
 3. Galen Burghardt & Terry Belton - The Treasury Bond Basis (McGraw-Hill, 3rd ed. 2005)
    Treasury futures: delivery, cheapest-to-deliver, rolls. Needed for our month-end bond trade (I-109).
 4. Robert Carver - Advanced Futures Trading Strategies (Harriman House 2023)
    30 tested futures strategies with costs, carry, roll; from the author of a book we already use.
 5. Larry Harris - Trading and Exchanges (Oxford 2003)
    Who trades with whom and why; the basic book on order flow and dealers.
 6. Bouchaud, Bonart, Donier, Gould - Trades, Quotes and Prices (Cambridge 2018)
    Price impact of large orders, with data. Explains how big forced orders move prices.
 7. Marcy Stigum & Anthony Crescenzi - Stigum's Money Market (McGraw-Hill, 4th ed. 2007)
    Repo, T-bills, month-end and quarter-end funding: forced flows in money markets.
 8. Ananth Madhavan - Exchange-Traded Funds and the New Dynamics of Investing (Oxford 2016)
    ETF creation / redemption and the flows they force in shares and futures.
 9. Dave Abner - The ETF Handbook (Wiley, 2nd ed. 2016)
    The practical side of ETF mechanics and rebalancing.
10. Brent Donnelly - The Art of Currency Trading (Wiley 2019)
    FX from a bank trader: fixings, month-end flows, positioning. Fits our FTMO currency pairs.
11. Antti Ilmanen - Investing Amid Low Expected Returns (Wiley 2022)
    Update of book 1 with newer evidence.
12. Kakushadze & Serur - 151 Trading Strategies (Palgrave 2018)  [free SSRN preprint; SSRN blocks scripts]
    A catalogue of strategies with formulas and references. CAUTION: Springer marks the book RETRACTED (reason
    not found); only as an idea list. Optional: download the SSRN preprint by hand (papers.ssrn.com, id 3247865).
