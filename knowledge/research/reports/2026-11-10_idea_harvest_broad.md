# Broad idea harvest — 2026-11-10

Research leads, not trading specifications or tested edges. Ranked by judgment of potential to clear costs, considering evidence, trade size and implementation; ranks are not probabilities. No development or holdout backtest was run. Each recipe below is an explicitly proposed hypothesis, not a change to an existing trading rule. PUBLISHED means the underlying effect is published, PRACTITIONER a documented rule, and SPECULATIVE a new adaptation; a label does not validate the whole recipe.

## Common conventions and cost lens

Use completed candles only, next available M1 open after a signal, and executable bid/ask plus slippage. D1 is 17:00 New York to 17:00 New York on trading days for CFDs, 00:00 UTC for crypto; intraday event clocks use America/New_York. ATR is Wilder ATR(14) from completed D1 bars, excluding incomplete weekend stubs. Stops are fixed at entry, measured from actual fill; gap through stop fills at the next executable price, not the stop. No re-entry until flat; cancel pending orders at the stated expiry. All time exits execute at the first M1 open at or after the time, and missing data invalidates the event. One tick means the instrument's minimum price increment. For multi-day CFD holds charge actual side-specific daily swap, including triple days; for perps charge actual signed funding at each settlement held, exchange fees and slippage. US500 uses the actual exchange holiday/early-close calendar; signals after the last tradable bar wait for the next executable bar. No same-close execution with knowledge of that close.

The move ranges below are **rough scenario magnitudes for absolute entry-to-exit price movement**, not expected signed gross profit, study results, or forecasts. They are unmeasured orders of magnitude and must be replaced with development measurements. Break-even requires signed mean gross return greater than all costs; wide stops merely change R units. Illustrative US500/major FX round-trip 1–2 bp excludes swap and stressed execution; energy, gold and crypto costs must be measured from the applicable feed. For perps 10–30 bp round-trip here is only a stress assumption, not a verified fee schedule; funding may be a cost or receipt. Trade counts are unverified per instrument-year, not summed or guarantees. Small samples and correlated trades make rankings fragile. The index financing model is known to overstate older financing; fix/validate price scaling before comparing multi-day index net results.

## 1. EIA oil late-session response — PUBLISHED effect, proposed trade

Source actually opened: [EIA release schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php); the 2021 Indriawan et al. paper's abstract was visible in search but its [SSRN page](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3822093) returned 403 on open. Its reported third-half-hour to final-half-hour predictability is therefore a search-snippet lead, not independently verified from the paper. Recipe: USOIL M1, scheduled petroleum release at 10:30 ET, excluding altered-time releases. At 11:00 take sign of 10:30–11:00 return; at 13:59 enter that direction, 1 D1 ATR stop, exit 14:30 ET. Skip zero returns and closed/missing bars. The time choice is a proposed approximation; verify the paper's precise session and signal before claiming replication. Information incorporation/liquidity may generate delayed continuation. Approx. 45–50 trades/year. Scenario 5–40 bp absolute move versus perhaps 5–20+ bp oil round-trip, more on news; mean signal could be far smaller. No verified net performance claim. Closely related to the already failed last-half-hour index momentum family; different oil/EIA event, and the cost wall makes it a high-risk lead despite ranking.

## 2. Connors 8-day reversal — PRACTITIONER

[Opened lecture reproducing Connors' rules](https://michaeltanphd.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/technicalstrategies_2002.pdf), pp. 26–27. US500 D1, long: day 1 low below prior 7 daily lows; day 2 trades above day 1 high; one of days 3–6 trades below day 2 low. Once that day closes, place buy stop one tick above day 2 high for four subsequent trading days. Stop one tick below day 2 low; reject if stop is not below entry. Exit after five completed trading days from fill or 2R target, whichever first; mirror for shorts. One active setup per direction; first qualifying day 3–6 only. These timing, expiry and exit choices complete the source's incomplete rules and do not assert its performance. Mechanism: failed downside extension followed by recovery traps sellers. Rough 5–20 trades/year; absolute move 30–200 bp vs 1–2 bp plus 1–5 days' swap. No performance claim found. Intraday ordering on the signal day cannot be reconstructed from daily bars alone, hence wait for its close.

## 3. Capitulation recovery — PRACTITIONER

[Same opened Connors lecture](https://michaeltanphd.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/technicalstrategies_2002.pdf), p. 28. US500 D1: low below previous nine lows, range above each previous nine ranges, and close in top quarter of range. At next bar place buy stop one tick above signal high; cancel after two complete trading days. Stop one tick below signal low; exit at 2R or after five trading days, whichever first. One pending order; no short leg in primary hypothesis. Source specifies the setup and stop but leaves trailing vague; the fixed target and timer are proposed. Forced selling followed by absorption could create a substantial reversal; a large range also means expensive gaps. Rough 3–12 trades/year, 50–300 bp absolute vs 1–2 bp plus swap and gap slippage. No verified performance claim; rare-event uncertainty is severe.

## 4. Oil inventory overreaction — SPECULATIVE

[Opened EIA schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php); a 2016 [Rousse and Sévi SSRN abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2874907) surfaced in search but opening it returned 403. The snippet describes partial post-announcement reversal, not this strategy. USOIL, standard 10:30 ET petroleum release only: at 10:45 compare the 10:30–10:45 return to 0.5 prior D1 ATR; if magnitude meets threshold, trade opposite its sign at next M1 open. Stop 1 D1 ATR, exit 13:00 ET or at stop. Exclude shifted releases and missing minutes. Mechanism is temporary liquidity withdrawal/overshoot; directional information could instead persist. Rough 5–20 trades/year; 20–150 bp absolute vs 5–20+ bp and potentially much higher event spread/slippage. No verified strategy performance. This is event-specific, not the already screened generic H1 shock response.

## 5. Negative funding with price recovery — SPECULATIVE

[Opened Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) describes funding and basis, not this directional strategy. BTCUSDT Binance perpetual, M1 and settled funding: immediately after each settlement, if the just-settled rate is at or below -0.05% and the last completed UTC D1 close exceeds the preceding D1 close, enter long at next M1 open. Fixed stop 2 D1 ATR, exit after 24 hours or stop. Only one position per symbol; do not use an estimated future rate. Funding received at entry settlement is unavailable because entry follows it; apply later settlements only. Mechanism: crowded short positioning may unwind while funding compensates holders, but funding is not a reliable liquidation detector. Rough 0–25 trades/year; 100–800 bp absolute vs assumed 10–30 bp plus subsequent signed funding. A 2026 SSRN search snippet claims conditional returns, but its page returned 403 and its sample is 2021–26; no paper performance is attributed to this rule. Binance perps began late in development, so availability and event counts must be verified before building.

## 6. Double Seven pullback — PRACTITIONER

[Opened coauthor Alvarez](https://alvarezquanttrading.com/blog/double-7s-strategy/) describes the original ETF rule and later erosion. US500 D1: when close above SMA(200) and no greater than previous six closes, buy next M1 open; stop 2 D1 ATR; exit next M1 open after a close at or above the previous six closes, or after 15 trading days. Long only, one position. Stop, timer and next-open execution are additions; original buys/sells at close and had no stated protective stop. Pullbacks in an upward market may revert, although this is highly correlated with the failed RSI(2) index family and is a low-priority retest of the broader pullback mechanism, not independent confirmation. Approximately 10–30/year; 30–200 bp absolute vs 1–2 bp plus swap. Alvarez warns the edge may have diminished; no transferable performance claimed.

## 7. EIA natural-gas delayed reversal — SPECULATIVE

[Opened EIA release page](https://ir.eia.gov/index.html) identifies the usual Thursday release; use verified historical timestamps, excluding shifted releases. NATGAS M1: at 10:45 ET, if absolute 10:30–10:45 return exceeds 0.75 previous D1 ATR, fade it at next M1 open; 1.5 D1 ATR stop, exit 13:30 ET. Mechanism: temporary imbalance around a concentrated inventory-information shock. No source establishes reversal or profitability in gas; it is a distinct untested cross-commodity hypothesis. Rough 5–15/year; 30–250 bp absolute vs perhaps 10–40+ bp plus news slippage. Data, broker contract mapping and release execution require checks.

## 8. Crypto weekend displacement reversal — SPECULATIVE

[Opened Bitcoin scale study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00395) documents heavy tails and scale-dependent volatility, not weekend reversal. BTCUSDT perpetual, 00:00 UTC Monday: measure Friday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 close-to-close return; if its magnitude exceeds 2 prior D1 ATR expressed in price, enter against its direction at next M1 open. Stop 2 D1 ATR; exit 24 hours later. Hypothesis: thin weekend liquidity can temporarily displace price before weekday liquidity returns. Rough 3–15/year; 100–700 bp absolute vs 10–30 bp assumed plus funding. No performance claim. The 2026 [weekend-volatility paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35702) finds predictive content weak before 2024 and predicts variance, not direction; it does not establish this trade.

## 9. Oil post-release continuation — SPECULATIVE

[Opened EIA schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php). USOIL on standard releases: 10:30–11:00 ET return at least 0.5 prior D1 ATR in magnitude; enter its direction at 11:00 next M1 open, stop 1 D1 ATR, exit 14:00 ET. Informed repricing may continue over hours; opposite to #4 but a different half-hour signal and three-hour holding window. No performance claim, and it must be treated as a separate trial, not selected after comparing with the fade. Rough 10–25/year; 20–150 bp absolute vs 5–20+ bp and news slippage. Verify historical release calendar. The source supports release timing only.

## 10. Crypto funding-pressure continuation — SPECULATIVE

[Opened perpetual-futures paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) explains the funding mechanism; it does not establish directional continuation. BTCUSDT perpetual: after settlement, if settled funding <= -0.05% and latest completed UTC D1 close is below previous D1 close, enter short at next M1 open; stop 2 D1 ATR; exit after 24 hours. Exclude zero daily change, one position. Mechanism: negative funding and falling price may coincide with persistent deleveraging; short pays subsequent negative funding and the signal could mark exhaustion. Rough 0–25/year; 100–800 bp absolute vs 10–30 bp assumed plus funding. No verified performance. Compare with unfiltered down days and #5, and count both as distinct trials.

## 11. Weekend volatility regime with gold pullback — SPECULATIVE

[Opened 2026 arXiv study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35702) finds weekend crypto variance predicts Monday gold variance mainly after 2024, not returns. At Monday 08:00 ET compute BTCUSDT Saturday+Sunday realized variance from completed hourly log returns; require it to exceed median of previous 20 weekends. On XAUUSD at 08:00, compare last completed H1 close with 06:00 ET close; if down by at least 0.25 prior D1 ATR, buy; if up by at least 0.25 ATR, sell; otherwise skip. Stop 1 D1 ATR; exit 14:00 ET. Regime may concentrate dislocations; directional reversal is unsupported and the cited post-2024 effect is explicitly weak in the 2015–21 development period. Rough 5–20/year; 15–100 bp absolute vs perhaps 3–15+ bp gold round-trip. No strategy performance claim; BTC history and synchronized bars must exist. This is not a generic calendar-by-clock drift screen, but is highly exploratory.

## 12. Post-cascade recovery proxy — SPECULATIVE

[Opened liquidation-cascade study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27070) finds event heterogeneity and says price variance is not a specific early warning; funding is too coarse for intraday early warning. BTCUSDT perpetual: after any completed H4 candle with close-to-open return <= -2 prior D1 ATR in price units and close in top 25% of its high–low range, buy next M1 open; stop one D1 ATR below entry; exit after 24 hours. One position, zero-range candles excluded. Mechanism: forced-sale exhaustion; OHLC alone cannot identify an actual liquidation cascade. Rough 0–10/year; 100–1000 bp absolute vs 10–30 bp assumed plus funding and severe tail slippage. No profitability claim. This is a crypto H4 recovery pattern, distinct from the tested H1 shock screen, but the same multiple-testing danger applies.

## Interpretation and next steps

The top five are #1 oil late-session response, #2 8-day reversal, #3 capitulation, #4 oil overreaction, and #5 negative funding with recovery. #1 has only an inaccessible paper abstract and an unverified market-session mapping; #2–3 rely on old practitioner patterns with explicit new exits; #4 and #5 are conjectural. None is ready to assert as an edge. The most promising apparent moves are absolute volatility, not mean returns. Source claims based on optimized rules or extreme crypto samples risk selection and curve fitting, including exact funding and ATR cutoffs here, which are illustrative hypotheses rather than evidence.

Before any build: verify historical oil/gas release timestamps, 2015–21 coverage and symbol continuity, realistic stressed spread/slippage and funding timestamps; define executable sessions and stop gap behavior; pre-register one primary variant, matched non-event/control days, placebo directions and costs doubled; keep all twelve in the trial count and test only development before any separately authorized MT5 holdout. Note the crypto studies after 2021 may describe later regimes and are not independent validation of our earlier sample. SSRN 403 responses are explicitly recorded above; no inaccessible paper is represented as opened.
