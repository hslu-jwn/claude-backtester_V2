Date: 2026-10-29  
Purpose: multiplicity and research-priority control; not an evidence review or strategy approval.

## Decision rule

A renamed trigger, filter, exit, or sizing rule is not a new independent hypothesis when its
directional prediction and event family are already represented by a failed or frozen test.
Overlapping items remain counted with their parent family and cannot be used to restart the same
development search. Management modules may reshape a positive edge but cannot supply one.

This map classifies the frozen local backlog under the current constraints: V2-supported data,
day-flat by default, causal OHLC/tick-volume inputs, stop-defined R for executable intake, and
cost-aware preregistration. It does not modify the frozen backlog.

## Specific alias controls

- **I-10 is not independent of I-07/I-08.** An inside bar after the first ten minutes changes the
  opening-range trigger but preserves the same opening breakout prediction. Its one-example source
  cannot justify another family.
- **I-12 and I-15 belong with R-2026-004/I-61.** Outside-bar fade and 3-ATR continuation are
  sign-specific versions of response after an unusually large bar. Testing them separately would
  hide the continuation-versus-reversal multiplicity the current draft exposes.
- **I-32 is not independent of I-33.** Bollinger-inside-Keltner and low short/long volatility are
  alternative compression definitions. I-34, I-35, I-41, and I-42 are downstream measurements or
  entries conditional on the same zone/break family.
- **I-38 is not a VWAP anomaly.** It is an opening-session rolling-mean entry and remains part of
  the opening family unless true executed-volume evidence and incremental predictive evidence are
  supplied.
- **I-44's positive A/B delta is not a signal.** Improving mostly negative ORB variants does not
  establish a positive base, and the reported test result faded. Volume can remain a reporting
  dimension for a future independent hypothesis.
- **I-54 and I-56 are filters on outcomes already observed.** Cool-down after wins/losses and
  structural-break/entropy switches create conditional subsets. Without an ex ante direction map
  and a positive base they are same-data rescue searches.
- **I-60 is an exit scale, not a prediction.** Sigma-by-horizon stops and targets can be compared
  with ATR only after an entry is independently justified; every multiplier is another trial.
- **I-62 already exhausted the no-wick retest family.** Its large parameter search and failed
  selected cluster cover the proposed candle/retest event. Adverse-selection evidence does not
  authorize choosing the opposite sign after the fact, and a renamed price-action concept remains
  the same family.
