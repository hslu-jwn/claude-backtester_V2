# Fast-lane family 19 admission screen

Date: 2026-11-06  
Scope: one bounded search for a genuinely distinct interior-session, OHLC-only, day-flat family
after R024; not an exhaustive literature review.

## Outcome

No R025 strategy or grid is justified from this search. The closest primary evidence is adverse
after costs/out-of-sample controls or belongs to an already counted family. Do not manufacture a
nineteenth family by renaming an autoregressive, moving-average, filter, support/resistance,
directional-run, opening, closing or shock-response rule.

## Sources actually inspected

1. Christopher J. Neely and Paul A. Weller, *Intraday Technical Trading in the Foreign Exchange
   Market*, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis Working Paper 1999-016B, revised 2001,
   https://s3.amazonaws.com/real.stlouisfed.org/wp/1999/99-016.pdf — **full PDF opened**. It uses
   half-hourly bid/ask FX data, genetic-program and linear-forecast rules, explicit transaction
   costs, restricted liquid trading hours, and an out-of-sample period. Apparent predictability did
   not produce excess returns after realistic costs and liquid-hour restrictions. Its rule space is
   dominated by moving averages, filters, extrema, lags and autoregression already represented by
   R008/R012/R013/R015/R016/R023/R024 or the parked level family.
2. *Performance of intraday technical trading in China’s gold market*, Journal of International
   Financial Markets, Institutions and Money 76 (2022), article 101481,
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intfin.2021.101481 — **publisher search preview/abstract inspected;
   direct page open returned 403, full text not opened**. The abstract reports 5-minute active gold
   futures from 2018-03-12 through 2021-03-10 and finds attractive rule performance disappears with
   data-snooping control, transaction costs and out-of-sample persistence analysis. The preview
   does not expose a distinct admissible rule that can be frozen without a new rule-universe search.
3. Atle Øglend and Tore Selland Kleppe, *How regular are directional movements in commodity and
   asset prices? A Wald test*, Journal of Empirical Finance 38 (2016), 290–306,
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2016.07.001 — **publisher search preview/abstract inspected;
   direct page open returned 403, full text not opened**. It studies monthly directional Markov
   dependence, not intraday executable returns. A duration/run adaptation would overlap R010,
   R014, R017 or R023 and cannot be counted as a new economic family.
4. Mathias Mesfin, *Structural Limits of OHLCV-Based Intraday Momentum Signals in MNQ Futures: A
   Systematic Falsification Study*, arXiv:2605.04004 (May 2026),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004 — **full 17-page preprint opened; not peer reviewed**. It tests
   14 five-minute OHLCV/OHLCV-plus-calendar families on 947 MNQ days from 2021 through August 2025
   with expanding-window out-of-sample folds and instrument-specific friction. None passes its
   joint net-return, T-statistic, per-fold sample, year-stability and permutation gates. Most rules
   are opening, gap, volume, session-range, event or raw-price mean-reversion variants excluded or
   already counted here. The manuscript also lacks an explicit holiday calendar and uses an
   unadjusted concatenated continuous contract, so it is adverse context rather than definitive
   evidence for V2 CFDs.
5. Harry Larkin and Andrew Larkin, *When, Not Which Way: Move-Occurrence Predictability,
   Directional Efficiency, and Regime-Adaptive Clustering in Foreign-Exchange Tick Data*, SSRN
   6919443 (2026) — **search abstract inspected; direct PDF open returned 403, full text not
   opened**. The abstract reports strong prediction of whether a five-pip move occurs within 120
   seconds from trailing tick features, but frames direction and monetization as separate questions.
   It requires tick data and does not establish an OHLC directional strategy.

## Admission decision

The screen adds adverse-prior context but no transferable, distinct mechanism. It authorizes no
draft, parameter cells, local outcome access, queue action, run or holdout. Resume family discovery
only with a clearly independent measurement/prediction pair or new primary evidence; otherwise
prioritize contract/result reviewability work.
