## Limits
- Alpaca closes are split-adjusted; SEC shares are as reported then. A later split understates the market cap and can push a firm into the top 5 % wrongly. Stage B needs unadjusted prices or a split table.
- Ticker to CIK uses today's SEC list; delisted or renamed symbols are unmapped (counted above). Share classes are summed.
- Event-window sd is higher than the non-event sd; the x1.3 column is the sensitivity.
