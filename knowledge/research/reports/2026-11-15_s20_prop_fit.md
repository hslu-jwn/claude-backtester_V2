## Reading

- At **25 % notional per side (0.5x gross)** the challenge passes in 90-100 % of attempts in every scenario, but slowly
  (~10 months; ~16 months with the slippage haircut). At 50 % per side: 60-90 % in ~4-6 months.
- The daily loss limit binds at larger sizes (a -1,335 bp day at 75 % per side is -10 % of equity).
- Optimistic: resamples the observed weeks (the edge is assumed to persist), end-of-day marks only (intraday swings
  unseen), demo fills at the firm's feed, and the firm's low-cap cap (HyroTrader: <= 5 % of the balance in coins below
  100 M USD market cap) is not applied; the weekly Strategy 20 book holds many such coins. The low-cap rule is the
  most likely blocker and must be confirmed with the firm before any fee.
- A challenge fee, an account and the firm's terms are the user's decision (money).

## Addendum 2: HyroTrader / Crypto Fund Trader published rules (read 2026-11-15) and a rule-faithful simulation

Read on the firms' public FAQ / rules pages (web-fetch subagent, quotes checked in its report):
- **HyroTrader:** low-cap = market cap < 100 M USD, or typical 24 h volume 0.5-5 M USD, or newly listed; **<= 5 % of the
  initial balance (incl. leverage) across all low-cap assets together**, else only 40 % of low-cap profit counts. Daily
  loss static on the initial balance, equity at 00:00 UTC (Two-Step 5 %); max loss 8-10 % (static / trailing not
  stated); no limit on open positions; hedging inside one account allowed; weekend holding allowed (funded stage
  stated); no single day may count for more than 40 % of the profit target; no time limit; Bybit / BloFin demo or the
  Hyro platform. Not stated: funding pass-through, basket / same-second order limits, challenge price.
- **Crypto Fund Trader:** 2-Phase max loss static 10 %, daily 5 % from the 00:05 UTC balance; Bybit funding applies; bots
  allowed (no HFT / tick scalping / arbitrage); weekend holding allowed; ~550 USDT perps on Bybit; **reverse-trading
  rule** (opposite positions open together >= 60 s not allowed; opposite trades on different pairs explicitly allowed:
  confirm before a long-short basket); $10,000 daily profit cap (a same-strategy basket may count as one trade). No
  low-cap rule stated. https://cryptofundtrader.com/faq/, https://hyrotrader.com/faq/rules/.
