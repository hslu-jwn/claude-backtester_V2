# R006 candidate-discovery saturation review

Date: 2026-11-02  
Initial scope: twelve independently screened territories and 37 traceable sources. The dated
update below brings the current total to thirteen territories and 38 sources. This is a research
allocation decision, not a claim that all possible day-flat strategies have been exhausted.

## Result

No territory justifies a strategy draft or local outcome access. The negative result is informative:
the bottleneck is not a shortage of parameter variants but the intersection of modern evidence,
causal inputs, supported instruments, and the M1 day-flat mandate.

| Failure class | Territories | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Adverse modern evidence or decay | Europe-open drift; pre-FOMC drift; pre-holiday premium; turn-of-month premium; natural-gas announcement-day short | Do not rescue with eras, regimes, calendar variants, or nearby clocks |
| Unsupported data, instrument, or execution horizon | Scheduled macro response; cross-market lead–lag; derivatives expiration; London gold benchmark leakage; EIA petroleum releases | Do not build expensive feeds unless independent evidence first survives supported latency and costs |
| Estimand does not transfer | Same-clock intraday recurrence | Cross-sectional constituent evidence cannot authorize a single-index rule |
| Evidence survives but current platform cannot represent it | U.S. Treasury auction pressure | Preserve as capability-blocked; do not proxy through DXY, equity indexes, or gold |

Treasury auctions are the sole territory that passes economic identification, fixed direction,
interior timing, M1 latency, and a modern subsample. It still fails admission because V2 has no
Treasury instrument or cost model and no contracted historical auction calendar. Adding those
capabilities would broaden the current CFD mandate, so this is a deferred option rather than an
implementation request.

## Allocation decision

Pause broad adjacent-anomaly search after twelve territories. Another generic calendar effect,
announcement reaction, benchmark fix, or intraday continuation paper has low expected information
value and a high risk of duplicating a recorded failure. Resume broad discovery only when at least
one of these occurs:

1. a new primary study predefines an interior-session rule on a currently supported V2 symbol,
   includes a modern untouched sample and executable costs, and needs OHLC plus a causal calendar
   only;
2. V2 publishes a materially expanded, versioned instrument/data capability declaration; or
3. the user deliberately changes the opening/closing, final-30-minute, overnight, or M1 mandate.

Until then, the highest-value research work is contract closure on the already frozen R004
calendar/result map, canonical result reviewability, and a typed diagnostic route for R005. No
rejected R006 family may be reopened through a local grid.

no broad discovery restart, proxy rule, draft, local grid, run, or holdout.
