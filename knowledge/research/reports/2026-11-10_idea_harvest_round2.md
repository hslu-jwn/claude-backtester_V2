# Broad idea harvest, round 2 — 2026-11-10

Twelve ranked research hypotheses, not tested strategies or trading specifications. This replaces the earlier broad harvest; its EIA, Connors and other ideas are excluded, as are the practitioner-harvest ideas and the failed families in the brief. Ranking is a subjective assessment of cost clearance, not a probability. The sources were opened; their evidence and our additions are distinguished. No repository data or account information was sent to the websites.

**Read the numbers correctly.** All counts and move ranges below are rough, unmeasured feasibility guesses for the entire stated basket per year, assuming available data. They are neither measured signal frequencies nor forecasts; correlated signals reduce the number of independent observations. Gross move means absolute entry-to-exit price movement in bp, not expected signed gross profit. Even a 200 bp move can have zero or negative expected edge. The >=30 bp move and >=50 annual trades criteria are unverified; some low-frequency crypto ideas are included under the multi-day exception. Cost assumptions: major FX and US500 about 1–2 bp round trip; cross, gold and other indices need measured costs. FTMO BTC/ETH: 6.5 bp round trip plus approximately 8 bp per night, side-specific actual swap and triple rollover required. Binance futures have separate actual fees, spread, slippage and signed funding, not FTMO swap. A 3-night FTMO crypto trade needs roughly 30.5 bp signed gross just to break even, before stressed fills. Wider stops do not increase the underlying edge.

**Common proposed execution conventions.** Daily CFD bars end 17:00 America/New_York, excluding weekend stubs; weekly bars are Monday–Friday aggregates of those daily bars. Crypto days end 00:00 UTC. Use only completed bars, enter on the first available M1 open after a closing signal or on a subsequent stop trigger with gap-aware executable fills. Exits triggered by closing indicators use the next M1 open; protective stops are active after entry and gaps fill at the next executable price. If both stop and target are touched within an unresolved candle, stop first. ATR means Wilder ATR(14) of completed daily bars, fixed at entry. One position per instrument; skip missing or invalid bars; don't enter if a protective stop is already crossed or invalid at the fill. Proposed levels and timers below complete source descriptions; they are explicit hypotheses, not claims that an author used them. Daily and weekly signal execution, CFD holidays and rollover, and instrument histories need a formal spec before coding. Do not use post-2021 material as independent evidence for a development-period effect.

## 1. Daily failed 20-day extreme — PRACTITIONER

[The Right Trader's Turtle Soup rules](https://therighttrader.com/learn/trading-strategies/turtle-soup) explicitly says no reliable win rate is available. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, US500 and NAS100, D1. If today undercuts the lowest low of the previous 20 days, the most recent occurrence of that old low is at least four sessions old, and today closes above that old low, buy next M1 open. Mirror for a high and short; skip if both signals occur. Stop one tick beyond today's extreme; exit at next daily close or stop. Skip a signal range >3 ATR. **Mechanism:** failed longer-horizon break can trap breakout traders. **Scale:** guessed 70–200 trades/year across basket, 30–150 bp absolute versus major FX/US500 1–2 bp plus possible swap; metals/NAS100 require measured cost. This is a daily 20-day extreme, distinct from the tested session-zone sweeps, but is economically related. No validated performance for this execution.

## 2. Three-day positive funding BTC reversal — SPECULATIVE

[MarketTrace](https://markettrace.ai/blog/funding-rate-extremes) reports BTC median **-118 bp** at 72 hours after top-decile funding, only 37 non-overlapping episodes in 789 days; this is an observational median, not the mean, a stopped return or an out-of-sample strategy. **Rules:** Binance BTCUSDT perpetual settlement signal, execute BTCUSD CFD if matched prices and coverage exist. Rank the just-settled BTC funding against preceding 180 days' settled observations, requiring 180 complete days; on first crossing into top decile short at next M1 open. Fixed 2 daily ATR stop, exit after 72 hours; one position; do not rearm until rank falls below 90th percentile. Record actual CFD swaps; alternatively run a separate Binance perpetual test with actual funding. **Mechanism:** crowded leveraged longs may unwind. **Scale:** 5–25 trades/year guessed; 100–800 bp absolute vs approximately 30.5 bp CFD for three ordinary nights, more at triple rollover. Source uses a two-year rank and hourly observations; our 180-day settled-funding rule and stop are unvalidated. Small sample, data snooping and BTC-specific selection are serious risks.

## 3. Three-day positive funding ETH continuation — SPECULATIVE

[The same MarketTrace study](https://markettrace.ai/blog/funding-rate-extremes) reports ETH median **+95 bp** at 72 hours after top-decile funding; its BTC result has the opposite sign. **Rules:** ETHUSDT funding signal and ETHUSD CFD execution using #2's 180-day rank, crossing and 72-hour timer, but go long with 2 ATR stop. Funding timestamp must precede entry; one position; no re-entry until rank drops below 90th percentile. **Mechanism:** leveraged demand may accompany persistent risk appetite rather than exhaustion. **Scale:** guessed 5–25/year, 150–1000 bp absolute vs ~30.5 bp for three ordinary nights plus stress. No evidence the opposite directions survive instrument selection, mean returns, costs, or our shorter rank. Separate trial from #2; cross-asset sign selection looks curve-fit.

## 4. ADX trend pullback — PRACTITIONER

[Raschke's interview](https://lindaraschke.net/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/august1997.pdf) describes ADX >30 and rising followed by an EMA20 retracement. [A separate test](https://edgelabtrading.com/blog/holy-grail-trading-strategy/) found 11 fills in 113.8 market-years for its strict daily version, and weaker out-of-sample results for a relaxed version. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, US500, NAS100 and GER40, H4. When ADX(14) >30 and rises versus prior H4, +DI > -DI and bar low touches EMA20, place buy stop one tick over bar high, valid next three H4 bars; stop one tick below signal low; exit 2R target or after ten H4 bars. Mirror shorts; one setup at a time. **Mechanism:** entering a strong trend after a pullback rather than buying the original breakout. **Scale:** guessed 50–150/year across basket, 30–180 bp absolute versus symbol costs plus swap. These H4 timing, target and stop rules are additions; the external daily study warns actual counts may be far lower. Related to tested trend following, but entry is conditional on a pullback rather than its tested continuous signals.

## 5. 3/10 oscillator Anti — PRACTITIONER

[An MQL5 implementation](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/24280) describes an oscillator state machine; it does not establish net profitability. **Rules:** same eight-market basket as #4, H4. Define fast = SMA3(close)-SMA10(close), signal = SMA16(fast). When signal crosses above zero, arm long; after fast crosses below signal, enter when fast next crosses above signal and close exceeds previous close; next M1 open. Stop at lowest low from the first fast-below-signal bar through trigger; exit on fast crossing below signal or after 12 H4 bars. Mirror shorts; cancel if signal crosses back through zero, allow one entry per zero-cross. **Mechanism:** momentum reset followed by trend resumption. **Scale:** guessed 60–180/year basket, 30–200 bp absolute versus costs and multiple swaps. The finite timer and exact implementation are proposals. Correlated with #4 and prior trend-following tests; results must be compared jointly.

## 6. Outside-day reversal — PRACTITIONER

[EURUSD outside-bar guide](https://www.thetransparenttrader.com/wp-content/uploads/Beat-the-Markets-Strategy-Guidebook.pdf) advertises an 88% win rate on a 2010–2019 historical test; examine its fill, exit and drawdown assumptions, and do not transfer that number to this different system. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, D1. If today's high exceeds yesterday's high and its low is below yesterday's low, and today's close is in the bottom 25% of its range, buy next M1 open; mirror top 25% for short; zero range skipped. Stop 1 ATR from entry; exit after two daily closes or 1.5R target. **Mechanism:** an outside day closing at an extreme may exaggerate short-term liquidation. **Scale:** guessed 50–150/year basket, 30–130 bp absolute versus FX costs and up to two swaps. The direction and exits here are research choices, not a reproduction of the source's claimed backtest. Closely related to other daily reversal patterns.

## 7. Smash-day recovery — PRACTITIONER

[MQL5's Williams smash-day discussion](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/21127) provides the reversal concept and extreme-based stop, not evidence for our portfolio. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, D1. On a close below each of the preceding three closes, place a buy stop one tick above that day's high for the next day only; protective stop one tick below signal low; exit 2R or after three completed days. Mirror for a close above each of the prior three closes and short stop below signal low. Skip invalid stop/fill and simultaneous opposing setups. **Mechanism:** a break back against a sequence of closes may signal short-lived exhaustion. **Scale:** guessed 80–250/year basket, 30–150 bp absolute versus spreads and up to three swaps. Exact three-close definition and exits are proposed; use #6 as a correlated family member rather than independent confirmation.

## 8. 80/20 intraday reversal — PRACTITIONER

[Broker discussion of Raschke's 80/20](https://roboforex.com/blog/education/trading-strategies-that-were-a-revolution-three-strategies-of-linda-raschke/) describes a one-sided daily candle, false extension and return; its point offsets cannot be transferred mechanically across CFDs. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, XAUUSD, US500, NAS100, D1/M1. When a completed daily candle opens in its bottom 20% and closes in its top 20%, the following day wait for a trade at least 0.1 prior-day ATR above its close; thereafter short on an M1 close below that prior close, at next M1 open. Stop 1 ATR above entry, target prior-day midpoint, exit at next daily close; mirror long. Skip if target already passed at entry. **Mechanism:** extension then failure can mark exhausted one-sided demand. **Scale:** guessed 50–150/year basket, 30–150 bp absolute vs spread/slippage and at most one normal rollover. The ATR threshold and execution are adaptations; no verified source performance. It resembles the failed shock-reversal family, so priority is limited.

## 9. Bollinger–Keltner squeeze release — SPECULATIVE

[TradingView's squeeze strategy](https://www.tradingview.com/script/G40dtEbK-Squeeze-Momentum-Indicator-Strategy-LazyBear-PineIndicators/) explains BB-inside-Keltner compression and release; the recipe below is not a replication of its momentum formula. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, US500, NAS100, H4. Define SMA20 and population SD20 of closes; BB = SMA20 ±2 SD. Keltner = EMA20(close) ±1.5 H4 Wilder ATR20. If previous completed bar has both BB bands strictly inside Keltner and current bar does not, enter in direction of current close minus close four H4 bars ago; skip zero. Stop 2 H4 ATR20; exit after 12 H4 bars or 2R. **Mechanism:** a compression-to-expansion transition might produce follow-through. **Scale:** guessed 80–250/year basket, 30–200 bp absolute versus spread and up to several swaps. No net return claim; closely related to tested breakout/trend families, and squeeze is a volatility observation, not proof of direction.

## 10. Weekly failed range extension — SPECULATIVE

[The Turtle Soup discussion](https://therighttrader.com/learn/trading-strategies/turtle-soup) describes failed extremes but provides no weekly performance; the one-week level below is our new hypothesis. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, US500 and NAS100, weekly. At each completed Friday week, if its high exceeds the previous week's high but it closes below that high, short next executable M1 open; mirror low failure for long. Skip if both conditions occur. Stop one tick beyond signal week's extreme; exit at the following weekly close or stop. **Mechanism:** weekly breakout failure could signal trapped participants on a longer horizon. **Scale:** guessed 100–250/year basket, 40–250 bp absolute versus spread and five nights' financing, including triple rollover. Weekend gaps and non-synchronous sessions create risk; it shares the failed-break mechanism with #1 and is not independent evidence.

## 11. Weekly inside-range break with timed exit — SPECULATIVE

[Crabel's original articles](https://nexusfi.com/attachments/72178d1335946495) discuss compression and expansion; they do not establish this weekly rule. **Rules:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, US500 and NAS100, weekly/M1. If last week's high is strictly below the preceding week's high and its low strictly above the preceding week's low, set next-week buy and sell stops one tick beyond the inside week's high and low; first fill cancels the other, expire both Friday close. Fixed stop 1 prior D1 ATR from fill; exit Friday close or 2R target. **Mechanism:** a multi-day contraction may precede directional expansion. **Scale:** guessed 50–150/year across basket, 30–200 bp absolute versus spreads plus up to five nights' swap. Frequency, move and efficacy unmeasured; economically related to ORB and tested trend breakout, though weekly levels and holding periods differ. [An external NR7 test](https://www.loomiai.io/learn/nr7-inside-day-toby-crabel-strategy/) found contraction filters worse than its unfiltered ORB; adverse evidence, not a direct test.

## 12. Positive-funding volatility regime breakout — SPECULATIVE

[Coinbase's perpetual-futures primer](https://www.coinbase.com/es-us/institutional/research-insights/research/market-intelligence/a-primer-on-perpetual-futures) suggests persistent high funding is associated with volatility and cautions it is not a leading price indicator. **Rules:** Binance BTCUSDT perpetual funding signal and BTCUSD CFD price, H4. Compute median of last nine settled funding rates and median of the preceding 180 days of settled rates; require the first > second and positive. On first completed H4 close above the prior 12 H4 highs buy, or below prior 12 lows short; enter next M1 open, fixed 2 daily ATR stop, exit after 48 hours or stop. One position; only one trade per break above/below that prior range. **Mechanism:** funding identifies a potentially active risk regime, while price breakout chooses direction; neither source establishes a directional edge. **Scale:** guessed 10–40/year, 100–800 bp absolute versus ~22.5 bp CFD for two ordinary nights, more on triple day; perpetual alternative pays actual signed funding. This is near the already tested trend-following family, so increment over a funding-blind breakout is the essential test. Parameter choices are speculative.

## Screening and limitations

Top five by judgment are #1, #2, #3, #4 and #5. None has demonstrated cost clearance in this project. #2–3 use later observational crypto samples, small episode counts and opposite selected signs; no claim of independent pre-2021 confirmation. #4 has explicit adverse evidence about very low daily frequency. The H4 estimates and all basket totals are guesses; if the >=50/year or >=30 bp criteria fail on development data, reject rather than relaxing the criteria after seeing outcomes. #2, #3 and #12 are multi-day crypto exceptions, and need complete historical funding and usable matched CFD pricing; Binance perpetual history begins late in the 2015–2021 window. Do not substitute an unavailable flow, volume or futures basis series with unrelated CFD tick volume.

Before building, pre-register one primary signal and fixed parameters per family; count all 12 and every variant, report correlated-event and per-symbol counts, signed mean and median gross bp, stop-adjusted gross R, all-in net R, swap, fees and stressed slippage. Compare #2–3 to unconditional and funding-neutral controls; #4–5 to unfiltered trend pullbacks; #9 and #11–12 to matched unfiltered breakouts; #1/#6–8/#10 to matched non-signal days. Use gap-aware fills, block bootstrap by dates, separate years and symbols, doubled costs and concentration after removal of best dates. Respect the development cutoff 2021-09-13 and request the user's go before the one-time MT5 test. Index historical financing scaling must be verified before multi-day net comparisons. No backtest was run and no implementation is authorized by this report.
