# Rule 201 trigger census (return-blind), 2026-11-26 (Sonnet worker)

No price return on or after a trigger day was computed or read. Script: `research/screens/2026-11-26_rule201_census.py` (`download | census`); files and outputs in `data/cache/rule201/` (`census.txt`, `census_by_year.csv`, `power.csv`).

## Data
- Nasdaq daily txt (2016-01 .. 2026-10), NYSE group daily xls (read with a small pure-python BIFF reader in the script, no package installed), Cboe yearly csv 2016-2026 (11 files). All anonymous HTTP.
- Missing: 103 weekday dates, identical for Nasdaq (HTTP 302) and NYSE (404). They are market holidays. Nothing else missing; 0 unreadable xls.
- Download incident: from 2025-06-16 on, Nasdaq returned a bot-protection HTML page (Incapsula) instead of data for 350 files. These were deleted, not bypassed: the script now treats an HTML body as an error, backs off, and fetches slower (plain curl, one request per 0.26 s up to 12 s). All 350 then came back as real data. The venv's Python lacks the issuer CA chain, so the script uses `curl` (TLS checks stay on).
- 1,346,718 rows -> 780,368 triggers after dedupe (keep the earliest time per symbol and date; Nasdaq carryover rows dated before 2016-01-01 dropped).

## Universe join and symbol mapping
- Universe = `alpaca_meta` + `alpaca_meta_fwd` (same top-1,000 per month, 2016-04 .. 2026-12; no month gaps, 0 key collisions). A trigger counts if its key is a member in the trigger's month. Triggers in 2016-01..03 cannot be joined (universe starts 2016-04): 2016 is annualised from 9 months, 2026 from 10 months (data to 2026-10).
- Key = upper-case symbol with every non-alphanumeric removed. Files spell share classes with spaces (`BRK B`, `PBR   A`), the universe with dots (`BRK.B`); both map to `BRKB`. Nine universe symbols have punctuation, all matched. Risk: stripping can merge a class share with an unrelated symbol; no collision was seen inside the universe.
- Trigger times are taken as ET for all three venues (Nasdaq/Cboe publish ET; NYSE xls not stated, UNVERIFIED).

## Power without returns
- Daily return sd on NON-trigger days (member days of the same names, at least 5 trading days from any trigger of that name; 2.49 m name-days, 2,418 names): 2.14 % (2016-2021), 2.40 % (2022-2026).
- Within-date correlation estimated from the cross-sectional mean of those days: rho 0.241 and 0.224 (not the 0.3 fallback).
- Date-clustered SE of the mean next-day return = sd * sqrt((1 + (mbar - 1) * rho) / M), mbar = sum(n_d^2)/sum(n_d) (trigger-weighted triggers per date), M = triggers with a next trading day.

| period | M | trigger dates | mbar | SE | MDE at t 3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016-2021 | 12,057 | 1,255 | 228 | 14.5 bp | 43.6 bp |
| 2022-2026 | 10,974 | 1,121 | 44.5 | 7.5 bp | 22.5 bp |

- Caveat (optimistic): sd and rho come from calm days. Trigger days are stress days. A rough sensitivity with sd doubled and rho 0.5 gives MDE about 125 bp (2016-2021, crash-cluster heavy) and about 66 bp (2022-2026). The check against 100 bp therefore holds on the stated method and for 2022-2026 under stress, not for 2016-2021 under stress.
- Not checked: the effect size would have to be measured on the trigger day close or next open; the census says nothing about whether a 10 % drop is a tradable entry (fill, borrow, the restriction itself).

## Verdict
GO to a frozen card: 224 independent universe trigger dates a year on average (>= 50) and MDE at t 3.0 of 43.6 bp / 22.5 bp (<= 100 bp). The card should pre-register the date clustering and a stress-adjusted power line, and should treat 2020-03 (about 3,200 triggers on 5 dates) as a cluster that must not carry the result.
