# Pre-FOMC equity-drift candidate scan

Date: 2026-11-02  
Decision: **reject as a current day-flat candidate; the published effect is a 24-hour
overnight/open-inclusive return and disappears in the modern matched-futures extension**.

## Candidate boundary

The FOMC meeting calendar is predetermined and the historical prediction is unconditionally
positive, so this territory initially fits the desired economically identified event better than
surprise-sign trading. Admission nevertheless requires primary evidence for a causal same-day
interval after the excluded 09:30–10:30 New York opening hour and ending before the announcement
and final 30 minutes, plus modern stability and cost room. The 24-hour literature cannot be
silently relabeled as evidence for that narrower interval.

## Primary evidence

- David O. Lucca and Emanuel Moench, “The Pre-FOMC Announcement Drift,” *Journal of Finance*
  70(1), 2015, 329–371, [DOI/record opened](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12196),
  [full preprint opened](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/60830/1/667832939.pdf).
  Across 138 scheduled meetings from February 1994 through March 2011, the S&P 500 return from
  14:00 on the prior day to 14:00 on announcement day averaged about 49 basis points and accounted
  for more than 80% of the sample equity premium. Prices began rising the prior afternoon and
  continued through the announcement morning. The paper reports a 1.1 annualized Sharpe ratio for
  the 24-hour index hold but not a CFD/futures strategy net of spread, commission, and slippage.
  Its estimand necessarily contains an overnight interval and the U.S. opening window.

- Alexander Kurov, Marketa Halova Wolfe, and Thomas Gilbert, “The Disappearing Pre-FOMC
  Announcement Drift,” *Finance Research Letters* 40, 2021, 101781,
  [DOI/full article opened](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2020.101781),
  [author manuscript opened](https://www.skidmore.edu/economics/documents/KurovWolfeGilbert-TheDisappearingPre-FOMC-Announce-Drift-200914.pdf).
  Using nearby S&P 500/E-mini futures, the paper compares 131 scheduled meetings in September
  1994–March 2011 with 70 in April 2011–December 2019. The matched 24-hour pre-FOMC mean falls from
  0.492% to 0.047%; ordinary-day means are 0.009% and 0.038%, respectively. The drift essentially
  disappears after 2015 for meetings both with and without press conferences. This is direct
  post-publication evidence against current unconditional transfer.

## Admission-gate assessment

1. **Independent estimand: pass.** A scheduled-policy-event premium is distinct from generic price
   shock continuation and does not need the realized policy surprise.
2. **Transferable primary evidence: fail.** The modern matched-futures extension has almost no
   excess over ordinary days and finds disappearance after 2015.
3. **Causal supported inputs: blocked.** A point-in-time authoritative historical FOMC calendar is
   not part of the current OHLC contract, though the conceptual signal itself is predetermined.
4. **Executable horizon: fail.** The supported historical estimand begins the previous afternoon
   and includes overnight and the opening. No opened source establishes a 10:30-to-pre-announcement
   return that the day-flat program could execute faithfully.
5. **Cost feasibility: fail.** The modern gross differential is about 0.9 bp before costs; the
   original Sharpe calculation does not resolve supported CFD execution costs.
6. **Modern stability: fail.** The effect disappears after 2015 in the 2019-ending primary sample.
7. **Bounded multiplicity: fail if adapted.** Choosing a favorable same-day entry, press-conference
   subset, VIX regime, meeting type, or announcement cutoff after the decay would create a rescue
   grid. None is authorized by the original unconditional rule.

## Decision

Create no pre-FOMC strategy draft, meeting-type split, or intraday clock scan. Do not transform the
24-hour anomaly into a post-opening trade, and do not condition on VIX or press conferences to
recover the pre-2016 result. The outcome is a recorded historical anomaly with adverse modern
evidence, not an invitation to test nearby windows.

Reconsider only if an independent post-2019 primary study predefines a wholly day-flat,
post-opening/pre-announcement interval, finds an unconditional effect across a modern untouched
sample, and reports net results under executable index-futures or faithful CFD costs.
