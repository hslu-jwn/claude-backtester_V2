# Research records (former research department)

Copied 2026-11-09 when the research department was folded back into this team (the user). The
full department (sessions, tools, fixtures, the shared interface, schemas and envelopes) is
archived unchanged in `../archive/research-department-2026-11/`.

- `records/R-2026-*.md`: one research record per hypothesis family (sources, mechanism,
  predictions, skeptic review). Candidates built from them: `research/candidates/`.
- `reports/`: evidence checks of backlog ideas (`I*_EVIDENCE_CHECK.md`), candidate scans,
  independent review packets, the discovery ledger and throughput funnel, the result reviews of
  the two tested submissions. Interface-only reports stayed in the archive.
- `research_registry.csv`, `strategy_registry.csv`, `source_registry.csv`: the department's
  registries as of 2026-11-06 (paths inside are relative to the archived department folder).
- `research_log.md`: their session log, 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-06.

From now on new research goes into `knowledge/ideas.md` (backlog) and `docs/research-log.md`
(skill `continue-research`); these files are history and are not updated.
