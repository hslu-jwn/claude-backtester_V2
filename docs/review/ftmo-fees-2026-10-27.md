## Currency conversion adjustment (new, FTMO 2026-07-28)

0.7 % of the **realised** profit or loss of a trade whose profit currency differs from the
account currency (USD here): a profit is reduced by 0.7 %, a loss increased by 0.7 %, applied at
the close, whenever the trade was opened. Not affected: symbols whose profit currency is USD
(USD-quoted FX like EURUSD / GBPUSD / AUDUSD / NZDUSD, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US indices, oil, NATGAS,
DXY, crypto). Affected: every pair with a non-USD quote currency (USDJPY, USDCAD, USDCHF, EURGBP,
EURJPY, GBPJPY, AUDJPY, CADJPY, NZDJPY, CHFJPY, EURAUD, GBPAUD) and the non-US indices (GER40,
EU50 in EUR; UK100 in GBP; JP225 in JPY; AUS200 in AUD; HK50 in HKD). Effective date "depends on
your product stage" (not published): applied to all years as a fixed rule, like the other fees.

## Other costs

- Swap: per symbol in the contract specification (points), triple on Wednesday for FX / metals
  and Friday for indices / energy (as here); FTMO changes them regularly. Swap time moves from
  00:00 GMT+3 to 00:00 GMT+2 on 2026-11-01 (DST): no effect on daily R.
- No weekend, holiday, inactivity or platform fees found. Swing accounts may hold over weekends.
