## Decisions for the user

**Decided by the user 2026-10-27:** 1 keep the candle close; 2 windows stay inclusive, sizes unset
while off; 3 option (a), exception written into `research-runner.md` > Periods; 4 the eight
suggested symbols; 6 a lighter Monte Carlo in the pipeline, the full one on demand; `orb_example`
runs to its end.

1. **News blackout timing (step 7):** the engine checks the blackout window at the candle close
   when the order is filled (`now`), not at the exact intrabar fill time (`trade.entry_time`).
   With intrabar M1 fills they can differ by a few minutes at a window edge. Keep (simple,
   conservative for a closing check) or switch to the fill time? Default is off either way.
2. **News windows** are inclusive at both ends (`[release - before, release + after]`); the
   window sizes per impact are settings with no values chosen yet (filter off).

3. **Spread table and the development period (full check, finding 7 in
   `docs/review/v26-full-check.md`):** the default FTMO median table (your rule, 2026-10-26)
   uses the nearest measured year for years it lacks: NAS100 / US500 in 2015-2020 get the 2021
   values, and 2021 is measured over the whole year, i.e. partly after the 2021-09-14 cutoff.
   That is cost calibration from later data in the development period. Options: (a) accept as an
   explicit exception (costs are not a trading signal), (b) development period uses only years
   up to 2020 / the fixed spread where no earlier measurement exists, (c) measure 2021 split at
   the cutoff. Opus leans to (a) with a note, or (c) if you want it strict.

4. **More symbols (step 9):** pick from `docs/review/symbol-proposal-2026-10-27.md` (Opus suggests
   six JPY / commodity crosses + AUS200, HK50); first `tools.mt5_info` to confirm FTMO's list.
5. **Cache repair re-runs:** after the step 8 fix the repair needs an inventory of the original
   days; the step 2 archive (now in `..\old\v26b-cache-backups\`) has none, so a re-run stops
   safely. Only matters if the repair must ever run again.

6. **Pipeline Monte Carlo is slow:** the end-to-end run of `orb_example` (6 variants, US500 /
   NAS100) spent ~2 h in the research step and 3+ h in Monte Carlo, all on one core (the queue
   gives each job `--workers 1`; the Monte Carlo defaults include 20 random-walk paths per
   symbol). Options: let pipeline jobs use a few workers (fewer jobs in parallel), or a lighter
   Monte Carlo in the pipeline with the full one on demand. Your call (no default changed).
